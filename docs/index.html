Private by default

Nothing about you
leaves your PC.

No sign-in, no cloud profile, no analytics, no crash reports. There are no AuraBrowser servers to send anything to.

  • Trackers and ad domains blocked before they load
  • HTTPS-only mode and tracking parameters stripped from links
  • Sites must ask before using your camera, mic or location
Tor, built in

One click.
Three hops.

Tor ships inside AuraBrowser. Flip one switch and your traffic routes through the Tor network, with no separate download and no setup.

  • Bundled and verified Tor, ready offline
  • WebRTC leaks blocked
Everything you need

Fast, light, and
yours to shape.

Passwords that ask first

Encrypted on your device. Unlock with Windows Hello, a phone passkey, a security key or a PIN — every time.

Sleeping tabs

Inactive and restored tabs sleep until you open them, so startup is quick and memory stays low.

Split view & Glance

Two pages side by side, or preview a link in a throwaway window that never touches your history.

Vertical tabs

A tidy sidebar built for wide screens. Collapse it when you want the whole page.

Your shortcuts

Every keyboard shortcut can be changed. Search engine, theme, text size and zoom too.

Easy move-in

First-run setup imports bookmarks, history and settings from Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox.
